Chung-Hsin Tseng (曾中信)

Associate Professor of Economics, Soochow University (Taiwan)

My research focuses on public economics, education economics, inequality, and applied econometrics, using micro-level and administrative data from Taiwan.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from National Taiwan University in 2019. I joined Soochow University as a full-time Assistant Professor in August 2020 and became a full-time Associate Professor in August 2023.
